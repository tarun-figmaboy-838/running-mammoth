sfx.js

Forty-five effects, synthesised in the browser and loudness-matched by measurement. Tap one. Change the key and hear the pitched cues move into it. Start the voice-over and keep tapping to hear the effects duck under.

Drop sfx.js next to your build and call SFX.play(name). A set key quantises sustained notes to that scale, so cues sit in your music instead of against it \u2014 pentatonic is the safe default, since nothing in it can clash. Glides are never quantised: a jump sweep is a gesture, not a note. Levels come from tools/measure-loudness.js, which renders each sound offline and trims it to a per-family target, so a UI tick sits under an explosion by design rather than by accident. Speech routed through SFX.voiceBus() ducks the effects automatically; Pack.speak() does it for you.
